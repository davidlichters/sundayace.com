How much room do you need?

About 12 to 20 ft (3.7 to 6 m) of flat ground. A driveway is about right.

Hard, flat ground works best, like a driveway or patio. It also works on grass, but the ball bounces less evenly there.

Keep the space clear of windows, cars and people.

It stays put when you hit

Fill the base with water or sand. Water works for kids. Adults hit harder, so sand is better, and it holds best on windy days.

Non-slip strips on the bottom grip the ground. When you are done, empty it and carry it by the handle.

Ready in 3 steps

1Fill it

Water or sand keeps the base steady.

2Attach it

Clip on the hook, then tie on the cord.

3Hit it

The ball comes back for your next swing.

Give it a few minutes. It takes a little practice to get the feel of the bounce.
