About SundayAce

SundayAce is an online store for people who want to play more tennis without needing a court or a partner. Right now we focus on one product, the RallyLoop™ Solo Tennis Trainer, so we can get it right.

Why we do this

Getting better at tennis takes a lot of swings. We want to make those swings easy to get for kids, beginners and returning players.

What you get

Every set has a base you fill with water or sand, a ball on an elastic cord and a racket. With 2 sets or more you also get the free e-book Learn Tennis: From First Swing to Mastery.

How we take care of you

You can reach us at hello@sundayace.com. We reply within 1-2 business days. Every order is covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee.

How your order reaches you

Free tracked delivery over $59.99. Delivery takes 6 to 21 business days. When your order ships, you get a tracking link by email.
