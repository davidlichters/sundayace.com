Questions

What surface does it work on?

Hard, flat ground works best, like a driveway or patio. It also works on grass, but the ball bounces less evenly there.

How much space do I need?

About 12 to 20 ft (3.7 to 6 m) of clear, flat ground. A driveway is about right.

Can I use it indoors?

Yes, if you have the room: a garage, gym or large room with 15 to 20 ft (4.6 to 6 m) of clear space. Keep it away from anything that can break.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery takes 6 to 21 business days. When your order ships, you get a tracking link by email. Buying a gift? Order early.

What if I am not happy with it?

You are covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee. Not happy within 30 days of delivery? Send it back and we'll refund you. You pay for return shipping, unless the item arrives damaged, defective or wrong. If that happens, email us a photo within 7 days of delivery. We'll send a replacement or refund you, and you won't need to send anything back.

Is a racket included?

Yes. Every set comes with a tennis racket, so you have what you need to start playing.

What age is it for?

7 years and older. It is not suitable for toddlers.

How do I attach the ball?

Clip the hook onto the base, then tie the cord to the hook. The ball stays on the cord. You need no tools.

Will the base move when I hit the ball?

Not once it is filled. Add water or sand through the cap and it stays anchored. Sand gives the most stability, especially on windy days.

Is it hard to learn?

Give it a few minutes. It takes a little practice to get the feel of the bounce.

Is it good for kids and beginners?

Yes. The ball comes back to you, so beginners spend their time hitting, not chasing. It is made for ages 7 and up.

What is the e-book?

An 18-page, full-color PDF guide for complete beginners, kids and adults. It covers the rules, the core strokes, 9 solo drills and a 4-week beginner plan. Read it on your phone, tablet or computer, or print it out. It's free with 2 sets or more.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship worldwide. Delivery times can vary by destination. International orders: any customs fees, taxes or import duties are paid by the customer.

Where is my order?

When your order ships, you get a tracking link by email. Can't find it? Check your spam folder or email us with your order number.
