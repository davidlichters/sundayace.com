Privacy policy

What we collect when you order from us or write to us, why we use it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who runs this store

This store is run by SundayAce, on the Shopify platform. Email: hello@sundayace.com.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, shipping and billing address, email address, phone number if you give one, and what you ordered.
  • When you pay: payment is handled by the payment provider you choose at checkout. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • When you write to us: your name, email address, order number and whatever you put in your message.
  • When you visit: information about your device and how you use the store.

Why we use it

To process and ship your order, send you the order confirmation and tracking link, answer your messages, handle a refund or replacement, improve the store and show our advertising, and keep the records the law requires.

Who receives it

Only what each of them needs for their part: our store platform, your payment provider, the partners who pack and ship your order, and our advertising partners.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart working, to understand how the store is used and to measure our advertising. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it for your order and for as long as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email hello@sundayace.com. We reply within 1-2 business days.

Children

Orders are placed by adults, even when the trainer is for a child. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page.
