How much space do you need?

About 12 to 20 ft (3.7 to 6 m) of flat ground. A driveway is about right.

The size of the trainer

Base: 8.07 x 7.87 x 1.97 in (20.5 x 20 x 5 cm). Cord: about 13 ft (4 m).

Where it works best

  • Driveway, patio or court. Hard, flat ground is the best choice.
  • Grass. It works, but the ball bounces less evenly.
  • Indoors. A garage, gym or large room can work if you have 15 to 20 ft (4.6 to 6 m) of clear space.

Before you start

Keep the space clear of windows, cars and people. Fill the base with water or sand, so it stays put when you hit.
